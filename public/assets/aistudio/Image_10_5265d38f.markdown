## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an institutional, sovereign aesthetic tailored for financial advisory, direct tax, GST, and corporate accounting services. The visual atmosphere projects absolute regulatory compliance, mathematical rigor, and fiduciary responsibility, avoiding the casual visual tropes of consumer fintech in favor of understated, high-credibility corporate gravitas.

The design movement combines **Modern Corporate Architecture** with **Precision Data Density**. Surfaces are structured, orderly, and calm, anchored by deep authoritative navy tones and warmed by disciplined metallic accents. Every element conveys security and institutional mastery:
- **Audience:** Business owners, enterprise founders, HNIs, and corporate leaders seeking top-tier tax structuring, audit readiness, and statutory compliance.
- **Emotional Response:** Confidence, stability, unflinching precision, and absolute discretion.
- **Visual Stance:** Pristine structural grids, razor-sharp alignment, quiet tonal layering instead of aggressive drop shadows, and high legibility across dense data tables, computation ledgers, and compliance status indicators.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around an authoritative deep maritime navy (#0F2942) paired with a tempered, non-gilded fiscal bronze/gold (#C59B27). Neutral tones prioritize cool slate over dead grey to maintain an executive, crisp appearance.

### Palette Application Rules
- **Primary Navy (`#0F2942`):** Primary brand anchor. Applied to top-level navigation headers, primary action triggers, hero headlines, and authoritative structural frames. On dark surfaces, it serves as the core canvas background.
- **Secondary Bronze Gold (`#C59B27`):** Reserved strictly for high-value focal points, compliance clearance badges, advisory highlight markers, KPI delta callouts, and bespoke interactive accents. Never dilute with excessive usage; keep it disciplined.
- **Tertiary Executive Blue (`#1E3A8A`):** Used for analytical visualizations, interactive secondary utilities, active states in sidebars, and informational callouts.
- **Slate Neutrals (`#F8FAFC` base, `#F1F5F9` surface container, `#334155` body text):** Creates a clinical backdrop for numerical clarity and tabular data readability. High-contrast typography ensures zero ambiguity in financial calculations.
- **Semantic Accents:**
  - *Compliance Positive:* Deep Forest Emerald (`#047857`)
  - *Audit Warning / Impending Due Date:* Warm Ochre Amber (`#B45309`)
  - *Statutory Penalty / Critical Error:* Structured Crimson (`#B91C1C`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for structural display headings with **Inter** for narrative copy, metadata, and numerical arrays.

### Typographic Implementation Directives
- **Heading Character:** Plus Jakarta Sans delivers contemporary authority with engineered geometry. Keep headings at negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.025em`) to evoke executive poise and eliminate loose visual weight.
- **Financial & Numerical Hygiene:** Inter must be rendered with `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "cv05" 1` across all ledger displays, statutory computation summaries, and tax breakdown matrices to ensure perfectly aligned columns.
- **Trust Seals & Attributions:** Metadata, tax agent credentials, and proprietorship marks use uppercase `label-sm` with widened tracking (`0.04em`) to establish institutional presence.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses a disciplined 12-column fixed-max grid on desktop (max width 1320px) transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet and a 4-column layout on mobile.

### Spacing & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 3rem (`48px`) outer horizontal margins. Content panels adhere to consistent analytical module ratios.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, 2rem (`32px`) margins. Sidebar controls collapse into an accessible top navigation rail.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, 1rem (`16px`) margins. Data tables switch to stacked key-value summaries with pinned status labels.
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows strict multiples of `4px` and `8px`. Structural module vertical gaps use `space-xl` (`40px`), card internal padding standardizes on `space-lg` (`24px`), and internal field groupings operate on `space-sm` (`8px`) or `space-xs` (`4px`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy avoids generic diffuse drop shadows, adopting instead a **Tonal Layering with Crisp Boundary Contours** methodology.

### Layering Architecture
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure Cool White (`#FFFFFF`) or Slate Neutral Light (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Surface Tier 1 (Level 1 - Cards, Financial Tables):** Surface fill `#FFFFFF` enclosed by a crisp, low-contrast border in `#E2E8F0` (`1px solid`) with a hairline ambient grounding: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Level 2 - Hovered Metrics, Active Consultation Drawers):** Boundary border shifts to `#CBD5E1` with an elevated directional drop: `0 8px 16px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Tier 3 (Level 3 - Tax Filing Modals, Advisory Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a dense Navy backdrop scrim (`rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.60)`) and structural shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`.
- **Trust Strip Inset:** Signature metrics (e.g., "Proprietor: Afrid Syed", "GST Registered Advisory") sit within slightly recessed containers (`#F1F5F9`) with an inset border line (`border-left: 3px solid #C59B27`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces a **Soft Structural (`1`)** shape language. Overly rounded or pill shapes are avoided to preserve corporate seriousness and tabular cohesion.

### Corner Radii
- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges, Chips):** `0.25rem` (`4px`). Conveys architectural discipline and clean alignment with strict vertical grid lines.
- **Containers & Structural Cards:** `0.5rem` (`8px`). Prevents aggressive sharp points while keeping visual lines rectilinear and compact.
- **Large Panels & Master Shells:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) maximum.
- **Status Pills / Indicators:** Restrained to `0.25rem` (`4px`) with bold typography rather than circular pill endpoints.
